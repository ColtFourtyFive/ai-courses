# Module 0 · Start Here: Demystify
Emotional target: fear → "wait, I made that?"   ·   Est. time: ~15–20 min

## Why this matters
The first thing most people think when they hear "AI" is: *that's not for me.* You're about to find out that was wrong.

Every lesson after this one explains how AI works. This one just shows you that you can use it — right now, today, before you know any of that.

---

## Concept

One thing to know before you start:

**AI tools respond to plain-English descriptions. You describe what you want. A result appears. That's it.**

No special commands. No coding. No setup beyond opening a chat window. Whatever you can describe in a sentence, you can ask for.

That's the whole interface. Let's use it.

---

## Mission

**Pick one. Build it. Read what comes back.**

Choose whichever one sounds most like you:

---

### Option A — Custom Quiz
*For: anyone with a topic they know a lot about*

Copy this prompt, fill in the brackets, paste it into your AI assistant, and hit send:

```
You're a trivia quiz host. Create a 10-question quiz about [FILL IN YOUR TOPIC — 
your favorite game, show, sport, artist, or anything you know well].

Use multiple choice: 4 options per question, one correct answer.
Make the wrong answers good — plausible if you don't really know the topic, 
but clearly wrong if you do.
Put the answers at the end, numbered to match.
```

**Example of a complete prompt:**
> *"You're a trivia quiz host. Create a 10-question quiz about competitive Pokémon battling — EV spreads, tier rankings, move sets. Use multiple choice: 4 options per question, one correct answer. Make the wrong answers good — plausible if you don't really know, but clearly wrong if you do. Put the answers at the end, numbered to match."*

The wrong answers are where the wow is. It knows your topic well enough to make tricky fakes.

---

### Option B — Rap or Poem About You
*For: anyone who likes self-expression*

```
Write a short rap verse (8–12 lines) about me. Here are the facts to use:

My name is [YOUR NAME].
I'm [YOUR AGE] years old.
I love [SOMETHING YOU'RE INTO].
I'm good at [SOMETHING YOU DO WELL].
I find [SOMETHING ANNOYING] really annoying.

Make it sound like a real rap — good flow, rhymes, a bit of attitude. 
Not corny. Actually try.
```

**What to notice:** it uses your specific details, and the result is polished. You described yourself in five bullet points. Something that reads like it took real work appeared in ten seconds.

---

### Option C — Story Opening
*For: anyone who likes creative writing or weird ideas*

```
Write the opening paragraph of a story in the style of [PICK A STYLE: 
Harry Potter / a horror story / a nature documentary / sports commentary / 
a fairy tale / anime].

The main character is [DESCRIBE SOMETHING WEIRD: a student at a school 
for time travelers / a detective who can hear what objects are thinking / 
a person who woke up able to speak every language except their own].

Just the opening paragraph. Make it gripping. Treat the premise seriously.
```

**What to notice:** you named something absurd. It came back written like published fiction. It didn't refuse or hedge — it took your weird idea and ran with it.

---

### After you read the output

You don't have to do anything with it yet. Just read it.

If something's not quite right, try this: tell it exactly what to change. *"Make the wrong answers harder."* *"More attitude in the rap."* *"Make the story darker."* You don't restart — you just describe what you want differently.

**Share your output below.** It doesn't have to be perfect. It just has to be yours. You made it by describing it — that's the whole thing.

> **Already comfortable with AI?** Try this: change one word in your prompt and re-run it. Notice what shifts. That's a preview of what Module 1 explains.
